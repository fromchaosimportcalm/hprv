## Which character you log in as is a raid decision
Date: 2026-08-14
Tags: playerbots,autologin,tanking,co,rule-1,roster,hprv

Context: `BotAutologin` is all-or-nothing. The module runs a bare `SELECT name FROM characters WHERE account = <yours>` and adds the lot; there is no per-character exclusion key, and the live config carries `AiPlayerbot.BotAutologin = 1` (playerbots.conf:151) alongside `KeepAltsInGroup = 1` (:148) and `LogInGroupOnly = 1` (:2177). So the choice at character select is not "which character do I play" — it is "which of the standing ten is the human, and which nine are bots". `Bullwark` is one of the nine whenever he is not the one. Verified on the box 2026-08-14: account 101 holds exactly ten level-70 Horde characters, and six of them carry a `co` row in `playerbots_db_store` — `Ararin`, `Nathos`, `Krast`, `Izri`, `Dijito`, `Ilyna`. **`Bullwark` is not among them.** He has never been converted, because until now he was never a bot. As a bot he therefore arrives with the full warrior tank strategy set: `IsTank()` true, `LoseAggroTrigger` wired to `taunt` at `ACTION_INTERRUPT + 1`, and `tank assist` pointing at the master's target.

Decision: **Swapping to a DPS or healer in the standing ten is supported and needs no preparation. Swapping to a plate character does not, and must not be done without converting `Bullwark` first.** The rule-1 fault is symmetric, and the repo previously only documented one half of it. Play a non-tank and there is still exactly one body `IsTank()` accepts — `Bullwark` — so the fault cannot fire, the global `+threat` cap keeps working, and `co -threat` stays forbidden. Play `Ararin` (or any other plate body) and there are two: him via the prot spec fallback, you via his tank strategies. He then taunts the boss off you on cooldown, exactly as `Crumm` did at 3,651 pushes for 49 executes. The remedy, if that swap is ever wanted, is the standard conversion whisper applied to `Bullwark` once, ever — `co -tank,-tank assist,+dps,+dps assist`. It is safe to leave in place permanently: `co` only governs bot AI, so it has no effect on the nights you play him yourself, and `gear-pass.sh` already refuses to re-roll his spec, so the rule-2 freezing hazard has nothing to collide with. It is deliberately **not** applied pre-emptively, because leaving him unconverted is what makes the DPS swap work.

Reasoning: The DPS swap is not a compromise mode — it is better equipped than the mode the repo already documented for it. `pool.conf` nominates `Netohje` as `TANK_MODE_BOT_MAINTANK`, and the same file records him at avg ilvl 115, quality 3 blue, dual-wielding: "fury-geared, NOT the crit-immune prot tank the old notes claim… cannot do that job as he stands." He is also on a `rndbot` account, so he is not in the standing ten and costs a summon. `Bullwark` is hand-itemized at 534 defence skill, crit-immune with 44 points of headroom, never re-rolled, and arrives free with auto-login. A bot-tanked night should use him. `Netohje` is retired as the nominee.

Two consequences follow that the runbook had wrong or missing. First, **the raid does have `pull` and `pull back` after all.** `docs/raid-night.md` stated that `AiFactory` grants them to tank specs only and therefore "no body in the raid has them". Verified false on the box: `Ararin`'s saved `co` list carries `+pull,+pull back`. This is rule 2 working exactly as documented and for once in your favour — the conversion whisper removed `+tank` and `+tank assist`, but `Save()` wrote his *entire* strategy list, which still held the pull strategies `AiFactory` had granted him while he was tank-specced. They are frozen in and they apply on every login. Second, **nothing advances the raid on its own, on any character.** Every movement primitive in the vocabulary is relative to the master — `stay` calls `StopMoving()` and clears CHASE and FOLLOW, `follow` releases it, `dps assist` walks DPS into range only once a target exists. No body scouts, routes, or pulls the next pack unprompted. Playing a DPS hands the boss to `Bullwark`; it does not hand him the instance. You still lead.

Alternatives: Convert `Bullwark` pre-emptively so any of the ten can be played — rejected, because it forecloses the good case to enable the rare one, and the whisper takes seconds when actually wanted. Subset auto-login so `Bullwark` stays out when you play someone else — no such key exists at this pin; the `SELECT` is unconditional. Keep `Netohje` as the bot-mode tank — rejected on measurement: he is 115 blue and crittable, and fixing him is a gear pass plus hand-itemisation to clear the 490 floor, all to reproduce a tank that already logs in for free. Move `Bullwark` off account 101 so he cannot auto-login — costs the main tank his auto-login on every normal night to solve a problem one whisper solves.

Affected: docs/raid-night.md, scripts/pool.conf, scripts/roster.conf, CLAUDE.md, decisions/README.md

Amended the same day — checking the box for this ADR turned up a live fault.

**`Rechiw` was never converted, and rule 1 has been firing in every 25-man.** He has **zero** rows in `playerbots_db_store` (guid 826, account 83), and his talents include Rune Tap (48982), Mark of Blood (49005) and Vampiric Blood (55233) — deep blood *tank* talents. With no override to modify them, `AiFactory` grants him the full tank strategy set from spec on every login. `CLAUDE.md` and `roster.conf` both listed him as converted alongside `Ararin` and `Crumm`, who genuinely are. **The blast radius is 25-man nights only** — he is on account 83, so he does not auto-login, and the sole plate body in the standing ten (`Ararin`) is genuinely converted. The remedy is the standard whisper, once, and it is gated on him being summoned: `co` is whispered to a bot in your group, there is no console path, and hand-inserting the row is not a substitute because the module writes it from the entire live strategy list.

**The check that missed him is the durable lesson.** `roster.conf` recorded "Verified in `playerbots_db_store` 2026-08-13: none carries `+tank` or `+tank assist`." That query asks whether anyone *carries* the tank strategies, and a bot with no rows at all answers "no" while running them from spec. It passed vacuously on the one bot it most needed to catch. **Absence of a `co` row is not evidence of conversion; it is evidence of the opposite.** Any future verification must assert the row is *present* and then inspect its contents — never assert that a string is missing. Of the 24 raid bots, 21 carry a `co` row; the three that do not are `Rechiw`, `Tanke` and `Anmine`, and the latter two are a resto druid and a rogue that need no conversion.

**Two smaller corrections from the same pass.** All 21 override-carrying bots have `+blacktemple` frozen in, not 20 as `CLAUDE.md` said — the claim itself holds at 21 of 21. And `Ararin` and `Crumm` both carry `+pull,+pull back` in their frozen lists, which is what makes `pull` available to the raid today.
