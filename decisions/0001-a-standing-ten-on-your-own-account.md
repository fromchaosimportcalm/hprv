## A standing ten on your own account, and a wider pool summoned by name
Date: 2026-08-14
Tags: playerbots,pool,roster,accounts,autologin,gear,client-limit,hprv

Context: The roster was drawn from the module's AddClass pool on `rndbot` accounts, and every session began by pasting two long `.playerbots bot add` lines because bots do not survive a master logout. The stated want was a rotatable 30–40 character guild/raid pool that stays geared, plus auto-login. Reading the module showed that three capabilities routinely treated as one setting are three independent gates: **gearable** is `IsAddclassBot()` falling through to `IsAccountType(id, 2)`, a plain `SELECT` on `playerbots_account_type` that works for any account; **safe from ambient gear churn** is account type 1 vs 2, because the ambient pool is built from `rndBotTypeAccounts` (type 1) only and `IsRandomBot()` additionally requires membership in `currentBots`; **auto-login and group persistence** are `BotAutologin` / `KeepAltsInGroup`, both own-account only. Two of those were already satisfied — type-2 `rndbot` characters were always gearable and always churn-safe, so the long-standing claim that persistence required leaving those accounts was wrong.

Decision: Put **ten** characters on Clinton's own account (id 101) — `Bullwark` plus nine bots — and leave the rest of the 40-character pool on their type-2 accounts, summoned by name. The ten auto-login on every session, which makes a complete Karazhan-capable 10-man that costs no commands: 1 human tank, 1 converted plate off-tank, 4 healers, 4 DPS. `CharactersPerRealm` stays at **10**. A 40-character pool is described in `scripts/pool.conf` with a home spec and offspecs per character, and `scripts/hprv-spec.sh` switches any one of them between spec and matching gear. Server-side tools deploy to `/opt/hprv/scripts` via `scripts/deploy.sh`.

Reasoning: Ten is not a preference, it is the ceiling. `CharactersPerRealm` is validated `> 0 && <= 10` in `WorldConfig.cpp:231` and documented `Default: 10 - (Client limitation)`; raising it is silently rejected with a log line and a fallback to 10. Worse, the cap is enforced only at character *creation* (`CharacterHandler.cpp:420`) and never at enum, so an account holding more than ten sends them all and the client answers "Error retrieving character list" — hit for real at 41 characters, which locked the account out of character select entirely until it was reduced. Given the ceiling, the nine bot slots are best spent on a self-sufficient 10-man rather than on a fragment of a 25-man, because a 10-man is a complete unit of content (Karazhan) and 25-man nights need a summon line regardless. Leaving the other 31 where they are costs nothing real: they keep `init=` gearing and remain invisible to the ambient churn, and only lose auto-login, which cannot be extended to them at any price. Weighting the 40 toward hybrids (16 tank-capable, 19 heal-capable) means a role change is a gear pass rather than a different character.

Alternatives: Forty characters on the account with `CharactersPerRealm = 45` — attempted, and the direct cause of the lockout; rejected by the client, not by taste. Dedicated `HPRVPOOL*` accounts for all 40 — works for gearing and churn-safety, delivers no auto-login at all, and is strictly worse than using the ten slots that do. Raising the cap by patching the core — a client-side limit, so the patch would change nothing the client can render. Enabling SOAP so `hprv-spec.sh` runs end-to-end — rejected after reading the code: `.playerbots bot` is registered `Console::No` and its handler opens `if (!m_session) { "You may only add bots from an active session"; }`, so no console transport can drive it and this is not a permission bit that can be flipped. Leaving the roster entirely on `rndbot` accounts — the status quo; loses auto-login for nine bodies that cost nothing to gain it.

Affected: scripts/pool.conf, scripts/hprv-spec.sh, scripts/migrate-pool.sql, scripts/deploy.sh, docs/pool.md, docs/raid-night.md, CLAUDE.md

Amended the same day — two findings from running it.

**Auto-gear on login removes most of the setup work, and rolls a random spec.** `OnBotLogin` re-gears any type-2 character whose level differs from the master's by more than 3, so a level-1 draw arrives at 70 in current-tier epics after one login with no pass at all — verified on `Gerina`, level 1 to 70 at avg ilvl 138. The spec is chosen by `RandomClassSpecProb`, so it still needs one `hprv-spec.sh` pass to reach the intended one. The same `> 3` gate is why migrating geared veterans is safe, and why a stale level-70 never self-corrects: six veterans sit below the current tier on gear from the old 125-era limit, and the level-1 draws now come out better equipped than five of them.

**The migration wiped `specNo`, blinding the spec-drift check.** `roster-status.sh` reads `ACTUAL-SPEC` from `playerbots_random_bots`, and the migration deletes those rows for migrated characters. `PlayerbotFactory` rewrites `specNo` during any `init=`, so this self-heals per character as each goes through a gear pass; until then the column reads `-`. Recorded because it looks like a broken report rather than a known consequence.
