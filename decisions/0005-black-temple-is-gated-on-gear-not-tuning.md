## Black Temple is gated on gear, not tuning — no HP restore until the Council enrage is beaten
Date: 2026-08-15
Tags: tuning,gear,blacktemple,illidari-council,enrage,autogearscorelimit,hprv

Context: The second Illidari Council attempt on 2026-08-15 ran cleanly with the roles filled (ADR `0004`), held for fifteen minutes, reached **24% remaining**, and then lost several bodies in seconds and wiped. That was not an execution failure. `boss_illidari_council.cpp:543` schedules `EVENT_SPELL_ENRAGE` on Veras at **15 minutes** from engage; the handler (`:573`) calls `DoResetThreatList()` on Veras and then `ACTION_ENRAGE` on the controller, which casts `SPELL_BERSERK` (41924, declared `:42`) on all four members (`:244`) with a yell. Veras drops his threat table and re-picks — onto a healer or a caster, never a tank — at the same instant every boss berserks. There is nothing to heal through and no positioning that survives it.

The pool is measurable. The controller is `The Illidari Council`, entry **23426**, level 70, `unit_class` 1, `HealthModifier = 700`; `creature_classlevelstats` for level 70 class 1 gives `basehp1 = 6986`. That is **~4.89M effective HP**, displayed as a quarter each on the four members via `SPELL_EMPYREAL_BALANCE`, with member damage forwarded to the controller in `DamageTaken`. Reaching 24% remaining at the berserk means roughly 3.72M dealt in 900 seconds — **~4,130 raid DPS against the ~5,434 the timer demands, a shortfall of 30–32%**.

Two numbers frame that gap. `AiPlayerbot.AutoGearScoreLimit` is still **141**, so the raid is fighting Black Temple in Tempest Keep gear while the instance itself drops 151–159. And `tuning/` contains nothing but a `README.md` — **no HP restore has ever been applied**, so 4.89M is already the *nerfed*, post-3.0.2 value that the BC-feel programme exists to undo.

Decision: **Raise the gear tier before touching Black Temple's numbers, and do not apply the BC-feel HP restore to Black Temple until the Council dies inside fifteen minutes.** The tuning programme starts in Karazhan, where there is headroom, not in the instance that is already 30% short of its own enrage. Restoring 2.4.3 HP here would take the pool from ~4.89M toward ~7M — a ~43% increase applied to a fight the raid cannot currently finish.

The gear pass is the documented procedure in `docs/raid-night.md` ("Moving the raid to a new tier"): raise `AutoGearScoreLimit`, `.reload config` **then** `.playerbots bot reload`, run `scripts/gear-pass.sh`, re-verify the tank defence floor by hand, `.save` before trusting `roster-status.sh`. Two ordering traps now apply to more bodies than that section assumes. Clear `co !` before the pass on **every** bot carrying an override or it logs in running the previous spec's strategies (rule 2) — and that now includes `Ararin` and `Crumm` if they were left in their ADR `0004` tank-restored state. Re-apply the rule-1 conversions **after** the pass, never before.

Reasoning: The gap is the right size for a tier. Roughly 30% more raid damage is what a full gear tier across 24 bodies buys, and it is the only lever in the project that moves that much at once — there is no bot damage or healing multiplier at this pin (verified 2026-08-13; the entire 102 KB `playerbots.conf` matches `multiplier|nerf` on two lines, neither of them a competence knob), so competence cannot be raised by config any more than it can be lowered by one. Attempting the fight repeatedly at 141 is spending fifteen minutes per attempt to re-measure a number already known.

There is also a second, smaller leak worth recording because it cannot be fixed from chat. Gathios re-casts Blessing of Protection or Blessing of Spell Warding every 15 seconds (`EVENT_SPELL_BLESSING`, rescheduled `:329`) onto a Council member, and because the health pool is shared, damage into an immune target is a total loss rather than a redirected one. `IllidariCouncilAssignDpsTargetsAction` only teaches **rogues and DPS warriors** to avoid a Blessing-of-Protection target and **DPS shamans** to avoid a Spell-Warding one; mages, warlocks, shadow priests, hunters, balance druids, retribution paladins and death knights keep attacking through both. Marking cannot correct it — the Council script rewrites each bot's RTI every tick — so it is a module patch or nothing, and it belongs with the deferred patches in `CLAUDE.md`'s open questions.

The stated goal of the tuning programme is *more* difficulty, and this ADR does not weaken it. It sequences it: restore HP where the raid already clears the content comfortably, and treat an enrage-timer failure as the signal that a tier is not yet cleared. An instance whose bosses are already unkillable inside their own timers is not measuring 2.4.3 feel; it is measuring gear.

**The enrage ceiling is a feature, and Clinton confirmed it as one on the night.** A raid night that runs clean for fifteen minutes and then stops against a hard wall it can only pass by gearing up is the experience this project is for. Do not "fix" the Council's berserk, do not cut its health, and do not read the alternative below as an option being held open. It is recorded as rejected.

Alternatives: Cut the Council's health to force the kill now — `UPDATE acore_world.creature_template SET HealthModifier = 525 WHERE entry = 23426;` is a ~25% cut to ~3.7M and would land the kill. **Rejected on the night it was offered**, and not on cost — it removes the ceiling that made the raid worth running. If it is ever taken it belongs in `tuning/` as versioned, idempotent, per-raid SQL like everything else, never typed at a console. Heal or play through the berserk — not possible; it is a flat multiplier on four bosses plus a threat wipe. Patch the enrage timer longer — a core script change rather than a module one, it would have to be re-applied across updates, and it hides the gear gap rather than closing it. Keep attempting at 141 and hope for a clean run — the shortfall is 30%, which no execution recovers.
