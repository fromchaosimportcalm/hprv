# Magtheridon's Lair

Map 544. `RaidMagtheridonStrategy` ("magtheridon") is applied
automatically on entry.

---

## The cube phase

Needs five bodies clicking Manticron Cubes on a timer. The module's
strategy auto-assigns one non-Warlock ranged bot per cube and reassigns
on death, so it is genuinely attemptable at 25 bodies — but expect it to
be the messy part of the night.

**Known module bug:** below ~30% Magtheridon may cast Blast Nova on a
~20 second timer against the ~55 second baseline, which is too fast for
Mind Exhaustion to fade from the clickers. Expect some late wipes. That
is not your setup.

Listed in `CLAUDE.md` under known-unfixable encounters for that reason —
attemptable, but it commits five of your bodies to a mechanic bots
handle poorly.
